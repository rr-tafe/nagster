Nagster — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Nagster is a personal reminder app that sends repeating notifications until you confirm a task is done.

Data collection

Nagster does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • The app has no internet access and makes no network connections of any kind.
  • All data you enter (reminder text, schedules, confirmation history) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries are included.
  • No data is shared with the developer or any third party.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data.

Permissions

Nagster requests only the permissions needed to deliver reminders: posting notifications, scheduling exact alarms, vibration, and restarting its reminder schedule after the device reboots. None of these involve personal data leaving your device.
